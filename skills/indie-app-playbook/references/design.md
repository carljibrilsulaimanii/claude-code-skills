# Making screens look designed, not default

Read when the user says the app looks flat, boring, generic or "like a template."
Each fix is a precise instruction to hand to their AI or developer.

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Looks **flat** | Design in **three planes**: big type in the back, the subject in the middle, buttons on top. The overlap is what creates depth. |
| People can't tell **what to tap** | Pick one **signal color** that only ever means "tap this". Everything else is ink and paper (neutrals). |
| Still looks **default** | **Skip the middle sizes.** One thing huge, everything else small, nothing in between. |
| Layout feels **boring** | **Cut out the main subject**, make it oversized, and let it overlap the headline. |
| Screen feels **empty** | Turn the background into a **scene** (gradient sky, texture, illustration) and layer content on top. |
| Feels **generic** | Add one distinctive style and repeat it everywhere: scalloped or shaped image crops, curved lettering, buttons in the same style. |

Paste-ready instruction:
> Restyle <screen>: use three depth planes (oversized headline behind, cut-out
> subject in the middle overlapping the headline, controls on top); one accent
> color reserved only for tappable elements; only two type sizes, very large and
> small; a scene-style background. Keep all functionality the same.

For paywall and onboarding screens, start from a proven flow
(`onboarding-and-paywall.md`) and apply these fixes to its visuals, not its
structure.
