# Making the creative (Stage 3)

Recurring ways creative production goes wrong, and the fix for each. All
words on and around the creative (on-image text, primary text, headline,
CTA, video scripts) come from the Stage 2 copy bank, already approved. If
production needs different wording, send it back through Stage 2 rather than
rewriting it here.

## Before producing anything

- Read the blueprint: approved angle IDs, approved claims, voice, visuals,
  who may appear, destination URL.
- Confirm which tool will actually render the asset (a designer, an image or
  video generator) and that it is connected under the right account and
  workspace. A tool on a website is not proof the connection exposes the same
  model or setting.
- Never ask for or accept passwords or login codes in chat.

## Static ads

**Inputs to collect (only what is missing):** shape (4:5 feed, plus a
separate 9:16 layout when stories and reels need it), fonts, HEX colors, logo
file, one to three writing samples, two or three labeled visual references,
approved testimonials or screenshots, photos of the person if they appear.

**Who appears** is the user's choice:
A. nobody (offer, product, real work, text, brand visuals),
B. original photos kept exactly as they are,
C. a generated likeness from 3 to 5 consistent photos with one primary,
D. a stated mix (for example three original-photo ads and two with no person).

**Process:** one complete ad first (image plus primary text, headline, CTA),
reviewed and approved, then the rest. Each ad is a separate downloadable file
on a different angle. A contact sheet or collage is a preview, not a set.

**Fixes that recur:**

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Nothing visible to review | Show the finished ad in the conversation before saving it anywhere. |
| Copy does not sound like the brand | Get real captions or a dictated explanation; name what is off (too formal, too pushy, words never used); approve one sample before the set. |
| Aesthetic feels wrong | Two or three references, each labeled with what is liked (color, layout, type, mood) and which one wins. |
| No fonts or HEX codes | Propose two directions from existing brand material, pick one, record it in the blueprint as provisional. |
| Face, hair, skin or body drifted | One primary photo, a list of what must not change, only named changes allowed; if drift continues, design around the original photo. |
| Wrong person or mixed references | Label every file: ME, OUTFIT ONLY, BACKGROUND ONLY, STYLE ONLY. A model from a mood board is never an identity reference. |
| Typos, wrong logo, made-up proof | Supply exact wording and the real logo; correct text as a targeted edit; delete any invented testimonial, dashboard or number. |
| Font or color not exact | Use the actual font file or an approved substitute; apply HEX codes to text and design elements; check at the size people will see it. |
| Blurry, crowded or cropped | Check the real file's dimensions; fewer words; placement-specific layout; never stretch a person to fit a ratio. |
| Every revision breaks what was liked | Name the version and the one change: keep photo, layout, colors and copy, change only X. |
| Wrong price, claim or next step | Recheck the offer and destination; image, primary text, headline and CTA must all agree. |
| Generation stuck or timed out | Ask for the existing job's status before retrying; duplicate submissions cost credits and create duplicates. |
| Got prompts instead of images | Ask for the actual files and a status list: complete, pending, blocked. |

**Saving:** only approved versions, into the folder in the blueprint, named
`<Month>_Static_Ad #NN.png` with `<Month>_Static_Ad #NN_Copy.txt`. Revisions
are new versions, never overwrites. A shared-folder link does not grant
upload permission; confirm the connection can write there, otherwise
download and upload by hand.

## Video ads

**Routes:**

| Route | Bring | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| A. Talking head from a photo | one clear primary photo, optional extras, approved script | one 9:16 talking-head video |
| B. Edit a recorded video | the original file, what changes, what must stay | one edited 9:16 MP4 plus a clean master |
| C. Cinematic 15 seconds | idea, feeling, references, any exact title, CTA or voiceover | one 15-second 9:16 MP4 |
| D. Publish-ready edit | raw MP4, platform, caption style, audio fixes, motion-graphics references | one polished MP4 |

Useful supporting material: 3 or 4 well-lit, straight-on photos of the
speaker (head and torso), and a 15 to 30 second 9:16 sample of them talking
naturally, for voice and mannerism matching.

**Audio choice:** the speaker's own recording, a clearly identified synthetic
narrator, or text and music with no speech. Never pass a synthetic voice off
as the real person.

**Process:** script, shot list or edit plan approved first, along with the
expected credit cost. One video at a time. Review the full video with sound:
captions, spelling, identity, offer facts, timing, the final frame. A script,
storyboard, thumbnail or generation prompt is not a finished video; if
rendering is unavailable, mark it BLOCKED and say what is missing.

**Video Style Card** (kept in the blueprint so the next video matches):
caption font, size and placement, colors, pacing, music style, edit
intensity, intro and outro treatment, aspect ratio.

**Common fixes:** the export must be the true 9:16 file, not the preview
widget's framing; when editing outfit or background, edit only the named
elements and keep the speaker's face and voice untouched; trim filler and
silences only when asked.

## Hand-back checklist

Before Stage 4, every asset in the blueprint has: angle ID, final file link,
matching copy, format and ratio, who appears and how, approval status and
date. Generated ads are not uploaded to Meta or approved to run by being
generated.
